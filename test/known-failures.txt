DEC-REX-ORDER	decoder-soup/0fb[cd]/opsize	-	a REX byte before F3 (not adjacent to the opcode) is ignored by hardware but decode() reads REX.W from it: 48 F3 0F BC is tzcnt r32 on hardware, r64 here
